# Personal Fitness Coach

A personal, offline-first Android app that plans and coaches your gym training like a knowledgeable personal trainer — built step by step through five approved phases.

**Where we are:** Phase 1 (fitness science) is approved; Phase 2 (product and architecture design) is waiting for your approval. Nothing has been built yet, by design.

**What's in this folder**
- `PROJECT_STATE.md` — where the project stands and what happens next. Start here in any new session.
- `DECISIONS.md` — every important decision, the alternatives, and why.
- `docs/phase1/phase1_report.md` and `docs/phase2/phase2_report.md` — the approved research report and the product and architecture design (commentable versions are the Claude Docs linked in PROJECT_STATE.md).
- `rules/rule_registry_v1.0.json` — the 140 approved training rules the app will follow, each with its evidence and confidence.
- `tools/` — small scripts that build and check the rule registry.

**The five phases:** 1 Research → 2 Product design → 3 Build → 4 Test → 5 Deliver the APK. Each ends with your sign-off.
