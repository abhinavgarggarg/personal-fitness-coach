# Impact of change set 4.1.0 (MINOR)

Evidence ratings that match what was read; source records corrected; health-condition table 1.0.2 (Phase 4 Part 4 evidence review)

Base registry 4.0.0 · 2026-10-25

An independent evidence review (Phase 4 Part 4) found ratings higher than the sources actually read. The Product Owner chose ratings that match (D-122): 14 rules whose sources were located but never opened become Expert Practice; pre-exercise screening (SAF-001, SAF-002) becomes Moderate (consensus); AGE-001 Moderate; the estimated-maximum rules INT-004 and INT-005 Limited. No rule's wording changes; the ratings change the coach's 'How sure?' answers only. SAF-010 moves to health-condition table 1.0.2, approved with these changes: cancer spread to the bones follows a supervised programme from the care team; pregnancy uses the stricter of the Canadian 2019 guideline and ACOG; a past foot ulcer or Charcot foot needs a doctor's OK. Source records are corrected: ACOG 804's three details actually come from the Canadian 2019 guideline (checked); the 2022 bone-metastases recommendations are read in full and the app now follows them for bone spread (condition table 1.0.2); ADA Standards of Care 2026 section 5, ACOG Committee Opinion 650 and Campbell 2022 are added as read sources; the FDA and EU summaries now include the exclusions that matter for this app; every source located but not yet opened is listed as pending.
