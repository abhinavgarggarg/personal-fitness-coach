## 1. Sources checked again (Registry 3.0.1, evidence only)
Nine sources were still marked "pending" at the Phase 3 gate. I looked each one up again on 19 October.

| Source | What I could read | What it says, for the app |
|---|---|---|
| Peterson 2011 (resistance training and lean mass, adults 50+) | The abstract (PEDro record) | 49 studies, 1,328 adults: about +1.1 kg lean mass, more with more volume, less with older age. Agrees with FL-003 |
| Fragala 2019 (NSCA position on strength training for older adults) | The full text | 2–3 non-consecutive days a week; working towards 2–3 sets at 70–85% of the maximum, lighter to start; power work fast at 40–60%; balance and walking work alongside. Agrees with FL-003 |
| LeSuer 1997 (estimating the maximum from reps) | Part of the abstract | 67 untrained students; predicted and real maximum correlated above 0.95. The lift-by-lift results are still to read (CAL-002) |
| WHO waist measurement protocol | Through a Statistics Canada paper that uses it | Midpoint between the lowest rib and the top of the hip bone, at the end of a normal breath out. The WHO report itself is still to read (FL-005) |
| ACOG Committee Opinion 804 (exercise in pregnancy) | Not opened: the publisher refused the request again | A summary of ACOG's earlier (2015) opinion lists broadly the same reasons not to exercise and warning signs. Three 2020 details the app uses still rest on the unread text |
| Campbell 2019 (exercise in cancer) | The abstract only | The 2022 bone-metastases recommendations (same group) agree with the app's caution for cancer that has spread to bone |
| FDA General Wellness guidance (6 January 2026) | The full text | Added as a source. See section 6 |
| EU MDCG 2019-11 rev.1 (when software is a medical device) | The full text | Added as a source. Wellness and fitness apps are not medical devices; the stated intended purpose decides |
| GINA 2026 (asthma) | The PDF stops before the exercise section | The asthma entry doesn't rely on GINA |

Also checked: the **ADA Standards of Care 2026**, section 5 (official summary). Its activity advice agrees with the app's type 2 diabetes entry.

Nothing the app says or does changes. The records now say what was read and how. FL-003 has nothing pending any more; FL-005 and CAL-002 stay marked. This was applied under the delegation for evidence-only updates (D-106, D-119), and you see it here at the gate.
